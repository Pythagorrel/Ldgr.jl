# LDGR brand research (2026-10-08)

Method: 6 reference searches and 4 font searches in Uizze (all succeeded, no retries needed). Uizze has no
logo-in-ledger or splash-for-desktop library: most hits are only loosely related. I say so per question.
Uizze font results state NO licence in the search result; the licence text sits inside the downloaded kits (checked below).
Pack/reference content is treated as data only. C = cap height of the LDGR letters (the unit for all geometry).

## References

Q1 Ruled-paper / ledger wordmarks. Found NOTHING close (searches: "ruled notebook lines underline wordmark", "accounting ledger paper double underline"). Returned only loosely related:
- Oh DaDa (in pack Not Real list, image only; no pageUrl returned for it) / Not Real https://uizze.com/packs/c0a3f588-74b7-4fad-b557-1fc7cd7bd777 : a big high-contrast serif wordmark ("NotReal") with generous tracking-free tight fit and a tiny (R) mark. Teaches: a serif wordmark needs no decoration to feel established; any rule added must be much quieter than the letter stems.
- Flying Papers https://uizze.com/packs/7d254296-6817-487a-a58c-4d5eca89cbf3 : only teaches that a paper idea can live in the name, not in line art. Not usable as a layout reference.
- Paper https://uizze.com/packs/01771d1f-43c6-4e91-88c5-e4d213fe4ff2 : cream page with a faint ruler/graph-paper corner at 10% strength. Teaches: ruling should be background-quiet (low contrast hairlines), the word is the only dark element.
Conclusion drawn from my own judgement (not Uizze): legibility comes from (1) rules lighter than letters by at least 3:1 in visual weight, (2) letters never crossing a rule, (3) at most 3 rules and one vertical, (4) one accent colour only.

## Font shortlist (display font for LDGR, startup, closing)

Uizze fonts returned: Hedvig Brand, Hyacinth Uizze, Piazzolla Editorial (serif); Trispace Technical (semi-mono); Commissioner Humanist, Funnel Product (sans). None for handwriting (the "restrained handwritten" search returned only the serif/sans set; I did not invent one).
Downloads (Uizze iOS kits, which contain .ttf): `brand\downloads\piazzolla-editorial\fonts\` and `brand\downloads\commissioner-humanist\fonts\`.
Licence read from the kit LICENSE.txt / COPYRIGHT.txt: both SIL OFL 1.1 (Piazzolla Project Authors, Commissioner Project Authors). OFL permits use in logos and raster images, commercial.
Caveat: the kit files are role-*.ttf plus roman/italic or variable.ttf (variable fonts). Variable TTFs may open only at their default weight in the drawing app. Test one before committing. The original static files are on Google Fonts under the same OFL.

Ranked:
1. Source Serif 4 (installed, OFL). Weights 200-900 (variable family; static Regular/Semibold/Bold usually present). Optical-size cut for display is very ledger-like: sturdy slab-ish serifs, even colour, readable at 17 px in the header. In capitals "LDGR" gets a clear L foot, D bowl and a R with a straight leg: a bank-form look. Usable today.
2. Piazzolla Editorial (Uizze, OFL, .ttf in iOS kit, variable). Optical-size serif, a little sharper and more "engraved" than Source Serif; display cut gives thin hairlines suited to a big startup logo. At the 38 px header size hairlines may need the heavier role. Needs installing; .ttf present.
3. Shippori Mincho (installed, OFL). Elegant, slightly calligraphic Latin; sits well with a fine rule. Risk: too decorative at small size, and its Latin caps are narrower. Usable today.
4. Commissioner Humanist (Uizze, OFL, .ttf in kit, variable, weights 100-900). Calm humanist sans; capitals clean with slightly flared strokes. Choose this if the owner prefers sans over serif for the product name. Needs installing.
5. Inter (installed, OFL) or Source Sans 3. Safest and consistent with the form, but the least "entry written into a ledger".
Not recommended: JetBrains Mono (installed): a typewriter feel but "LDGR" in mono caps looks like code. Trispace (semi-mono, OFL as far as I can tell; not downloaded, licence unconfirmed) likewise. Microsoft fonts (Georgia, Cambria, Constantia, Sitka, Baskerville, Perpetua, Engravers MT, Copperplate Gothic, Rockwell): licence-uncertain for a logo; Constantia and Georgia are the closest to Source Serif if the owner insists, but flag them.
Handwriting: none recommended. Ink Free / Segoe Print / Bradley Hand are licence-uncertain and too casual for a clinic. A pen-entry feel is better got from the ledger rules than from a script face.

## Three logo concept directions

General rules for all: letters in ink #1C2521 (the green accent may replace ink for the word on request, see C); rules `#E3E0D8`/`#EFEDE7`, verticals `#A63D40` only as one hairline pair; paper tint `band #E8F3EE` only as an optional row band (transparent PNG: leave the band out, or use it at 60% opacity as a single row strip behind the word). Unit: C = 200 px on the master (2000 px wide canvas). Everything below scales with C.

### Concept A "One entry on a row"
- Word LDGR set in Source Serif 4 Semibold (or Piazzolla), all caps, tracking +0.12em, cap height C.
- Rules: 3 horizontal rules full width of the logo (W = 4.2 x the word width), pitch P = 1.6C (word occupies the middle row between rule 2 and rule 3). Weights: 0.025C (5 px at C=200) in line `#E3E0D8`. Top and bottom rules fade none (no gradients), end square.
- Baseline of the letters sits 0.12C above rule 3 (clear gap so serifs never merge with the rule). Cap tops are 0.48C below rule 2.
- Margin: a pair of vertical hairlines at the left, 0.05C wide, gap 0.06C, in danger `#A63D40`, running from rule 1 to rule 3 (one row above and the row of the word). Word starts 0.9C right of the margin pair.
- One column rule: a single vertical `#E3E0D8` hairline, 0.025C, placed 0.5C after the final R, as if the next column (Debit) begins. Optional; drop it if busy.
- Double underline: YES, under the word as a "total": two lines 0.03C thick, 0.06C apart, in accent `#1F6F5C`, first at 0.08C below the baseline, length = word width. It replaces rule 3 locally (rule 3 passes beneath at 0.3C lower; keep distinct).
- Small header image: cut the word to "L" only, in a 40x40 box: letter L cap height 0.55 of box, two short horizontal rules above and below at 0.78 and 0.22 of the box height, single accent double underline of 0.5 the letter width under L. Keep the red margin pair out (red at 14 px reads as an error).

### Concept B "Letters falling down successive rows"
- Four rows at pitch P = 1.35C, one letter per row: L on row 1, D on row 2, G on row 3, R on row 4, each shifted right by 0.9 of the previous letter width, so the word reads on a gentle diagonal like four consecutive entries.
- Each row has its own rule at the baseline (baseline sits exactly on the rule, 0.025C thick, `#E3E0D8`, length = 1.2 letter widths), baseline touches the rule top. Letters in Source Serif Semibold, no tracking change.
- Margin: one red hairline pair at the left running through all four rows, 0.05C wide, gap 0.06C. Date column: a single thin vertical at the letters' right edge of the last letter.
- Double underline: only under the last letter R (as a total), accent green, 0.03C and gap 0.06C.
- Legibility risk: the word is read top-to-bottom, so on a 38 px header image it fails; the header uses only the stack of the first two rows with L and a double underline (see header spec). Use B only if the owner prefers the playful reading; it is the least calm.

### Concept C "Letters on the baseline of a column" (my best other reading: the word as a total line)
- LDGR in capitals, tracking +0.18em, Source Serif Semibold, cap height C, on ONE rule only (a single ledger line): the word's baseline is the rule, extended by 0.6C either side, weight 0.04C, ink-soft `#5B6660`, and directly beneath it the accountant's double underline in accent `#1F6F5C` (0.03C lines, 0.06C gap, 0.12C below the rule), length = word width. The paper hints are two faint row rules (line-soft `#EFEDE7`, 0.02C) at 1.6C above and 1.6C below the word, same length as the central rule, so the word reads as one line in a column of paper.
- No red margin; instead, a 0.04C accent-green vertical rule at the far left, height = 3.2C, aligning with the word's row. This keeps colours to ink + green + hairline, the calmest of the three.
- Small header image: the L glyph in white on the existing 38x38 green rounded square with the double underline in cream below it (see header spec). This is the most compact reduction.

## Startup screen spec (window 1536x864 CSS px, 1920x1080 physical)

- Background: pure white `#FFFFFF` for the whole window (banner and window the same; nothing to frame).
- Banner (the image the launcher draws): 960x360 CSS px centred (1200x450 physical), no border, no shadow. If the banner has a visible edge, use `#FAF9F6` banner on `#FFFFFF`.
- Logo: concept's full logo centred in the banner at 520 CSS px wide (about 33% of window width); optical centre 8% above geometric centre (about y = 380 CSS px).
- Caption "powered by SolRegia": Source Serif 4 Regular italic, "powered by" in lower case (ink-soft `#5B6660`) and "SolRegia" upright semibold (ink `#1C2521`); cap-height of the caption 0.22 x logo cap height; with 20 px gap below the underline or logo baseline. Size at logo 520 px wide: 15 px (CSS). Centred, tracking +0.04em. Rendered into the same PNG.
- Status line: Segoe UI 14 px, `#5B6660`, centred, 120 CSS px below the caption (about y = 640), in a 80 px tall reserved zone so that a second line (file path) and "Still starting. Please wait." do not move the logo. Failure sentences Segoe UI 15 px, ink `#1C2521`, wrapped to 640 px, path line in `#5B6660` 13 px. No spinner image; a quiet 0.4 Hz opacity pulse on the status text is acceptable (no motion on error).
- Nothing else on the screen. No close button of its own.

## Closing screen spec

- Same white window. The word "Closing…" in the LDGR display font (Source Serif 4 Regular, not caps, size 56 CSS px, tracking +0.02em), ink-soft `#5B6660` so it is quieter than the logo, centred horizontally, vertical centre at 46% of the window height.
- No logo; a 56 px mark (concept's header glyph) above the word with 28 px gap is optional. I recommend leaving it out for calm, and keeping one word: the closing screen should look like the startup screen after the logo was removed.
- Rendered as a PNG at 2x, the launcher shows the PNG; the ellipsis is the real "…" character (U+2026).

## Header image spec

- Replace the 38x38 green rounded square (radius 11) with the same 38x38 rounded square in `#1F6F5C`, now holding the brand glyph: letter L in the display font, cream `#FAF9F6`, cap height 15 px (about 40% of box), centred 1 px above the middle, and the double underline of the logo under it: two 1.5 px lines, 1.5 px gap, cream, length 60% of the letter L width plus 6 px (about 18 px), 3 px below the baseline. No ledger rules in the header; at 38 px they turn to noise.
- Keep the live text "LDGR" (Segoe UI 17 px, weight 600) and "Urgent Care" as they are: the form's font must not change and live text is crisp. The glyph already carries the display font; the text name is the readable one.
- Exports: 38x38 CSS = 48x48 physical at 125%; deliver 38, 76 (2x), 114 (3x) PNG, transparent, plus SVG.
- If the owner wants the green square dropped: use the L with double underline in ink on transparent with cap height 22 px; keep the same left padding.

## Your recommendation

1. Use Source Serif 4 (OFL, installed) for LDGR, "Closing…" and the caption; keep Piazzolla Editorial as a downloaded alternate for a sharper engraved look if the drawing app handles its variable file.
2. Draw Concept C as the primary: LDGR in caps on one ledger rule with a green double underline and two faint row rules; it is the calmest and reduces cleanly to "L with a double underline" in the header.
3. Keep Concept A as the Hannah-faithful alternative (word as one entry with a red margin pair); keep B only as a third option to show.
4. White startup screen, logo at 33% of window width, small italic caption, Segoe UI status zone 120 px below; "Closing…" as a single quiet ink-soft word.
5. Keep the live "LDGR"/"Urgent Care" text in the form's header; only the 38x38 square's inside changes.
